FAQ

Which sizes do you ship?

EU is the size in the box. The size guide shows a general UK and US conversion. Brands can differ by about half a size.

How do I pay?

Card and Mobile Money go through Paystack. MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, and AirtelTigo Money use the number on the checkout form. Pay on delivery is available when the store has it turned on.

What is the Mobile Money test number?

In Paystack test mode, MTN 0551234987 approves without a PIN or OTP. Do not send real money to a test key.

Can I review a shoe I have not bought?

No. Reviews are limited to verified purchases and stay pending until they are checked.

Do you hold stock while I pay?

Card checkout holds the pair for a short window. Mobile Money holds it for a few minutes. If the payment fails or expires, the size goes back on sale.
